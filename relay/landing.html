sigil relay_

A blind mailbox for remote approvals.

This is a relay for Sigil, the instrument that gates your command-line secrets behind a tap on your phone.

It is deliberately powerless. It moves sealed envelopes it cannot open, addressed by hashes it cannot reverse. It never sees a secret, a command, an account, or who you are. It stores nothing: a message lives in memory only long enough to hand it to the other side, then it is gone.

That is why it is safe to run wide open. No accounts, no passwords, nothing to configure or trust, because there is nothing here to protect. The security is in the sealing, not the server.
